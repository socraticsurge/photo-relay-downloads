local transfer ledger. It does not promise permanent backup or archival

Photo Relay and its plugin do not delete Google Photos originals. The cleanup

alone move an item to Google Photos Trash. That action can affect synced
devices and may become irreversible after Google's retention period, so verify
the exact video first.
